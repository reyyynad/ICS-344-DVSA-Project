Lesson 3 — Sensitive Data Exposure  (documented-only)

Owner   : team member responsible for lessons 3–6 / 8 / 10.
Target  : DVSA-ADMIN-GET-RECEIPT Lambda + dvsa-receipts-bucket-* S3.
Primitive: direct `aws lambda invoke --function-name DVSA-ADMIN-GET-RECEIPT
           --payload '{"order-id":"<any>","year":"2026","month":"04",
           "day":"18"}'` returns a signed S3 URL to a ZIP of every
           receipt for that day — no authorization check at all.
Fix     : enforce cognito:groups == admin inside the handler; scope the
          signed URL to a single order; restrict lambda:InvokeFunction
          to the admin role only.
